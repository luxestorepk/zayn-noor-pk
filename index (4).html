About us

Tradition, tailored for today

Born from the first light of morning

Our artisans give every piece the same care, from choosing the fabric and placing the embroidery to the final stitch. Each suit goes through two quality checks before it is packed for you.

How to measure

  • Chest: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.
  • Waist: measure around your natural waistline.
  • Hip: measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Between two sizes? Choose the larger one for a relaxed fit.

Not sure? Send us your measurements on WhatsApp and we will suggest the right size.

Shipping and returns

Clear, simple policies

Delivery

We deliver all over Pakistan in 3 to 5 working days. You will receive your tracking updates on WhatsApp once your parcel is dispatched.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders over Rs. 10,000. For smaller orders a flat charge of Rs. 250 applies.

Payment

Pay by Cash on Delivery, Easypaisa or JazzCash. For wallet payments, send the screenshot on WhatsApp so we can confirm your order.

Exchange policy

  • Exchange within 7 days of delivery.
  • The suit must be unused, unwashed and in its original packing.
  • If we sent a wrong or damaged item, we replace it at no cost.

How to request an exchange

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and photos. Our team will guide you through the next steps.

FAQs

Quick answers to common questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a colour and size on any suit, tap Add to cart, then go to checkout. Enter your delivery details and tap Place order. Your order reaches us on WhatsApp for confirmation.

How long does delivery take?

3 to 5 working days in all major cities. Remote areas can take a day or two longer.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available all over Pakistan.

How do I pay with Easypaisa or JazzCash?

Select the option at checkout, send the amount to the account shown, then share the payment screenshot with us on WhatsApp.

Can I exchange or return a suit?

You can exchange within 7 days of delivery if the suit is unused and in its original packing.

How do I choose my size?

Check our size guide page. If you are unsure, send your measurements on WhatsApp and we will help.

Will the colour match the photos?

We photograph every suit in natural light. Slight differences can occur depending on your screen.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible, before your parcel is dispatched.

Privacy policy

How we handle your information

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, mobile number, city and delivery address, and optionally your email. We use this only to process and deliver your order and to contact you about it.

How we use it

Your details are sent to our team on WhatsApp so we can confirm and dispatch your order. We never sell your personal information.

Storage on your device

Your cart, wishlist and recent orders are saved in your own browser so they are still there when you return. You can clear them any time from your browser settings.

Contact

For any privacy question, message us on WhatsApp.
